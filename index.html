About Me

Who I Am

I am an architecture student and designer working at the intersection of spatial design, information technology, and digital fabrication. I combine computational and parametric design, BIM, coding, CNC fabrication, and hands-on experimentation to transform ideas into precise, efficient, and inventive built outcomes. By connecting digital workflows with physical making, I test, refine, and realize design concepts with greater control, adaptability, and material awareness. This integrated approach enables me to bridge disciplines, uncover new possibilities, and challenge conventional approaches to design and construction.
